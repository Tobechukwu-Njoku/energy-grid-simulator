dataset identity/name, formats, access, licence name/link, and 132 kV circuits.

## Corrections and findings

1. **High — UKPN CIM URL/identity obsolete: six corrected bundles.** The old
   `long-term-development-statement-ltds-common-information-model-cim` page and
   catalogue lookup return 404. Current live catalogue identity is `ukpn-ltds-cim`,
   titled **Long Term Development Statement - Common Information Model (CIM)**.
   Corrected URL and identity for three areas. These two categories describe the
   same underlying locator defect; the declared counting method makes that explicit.

2. **High — UKPN CIM does not inherit tabular CC BY/access: six corrected bundles.**
   Metadata names **UK Power Networks Shared Data Licence - Common Information
   Model**, links its SharePoint document, and requires registration, login and a
   shared-data request. The licence link redirects to Microsoft sign-in: its body
   and share-alike status are **UNVERIFIED**. Tagged CIM **research-only / do not
   use pending clearance**; separately verified tabular/site metadata remains
   CC BY 4.0 / **open-commercial**. Public metadata is not model-file access.

3. **High — SPEN CIM has shared terms/additional request: four corrected bundles.**
   SPM/SPD descriptions explicitly require an access request and shared-data terms;
   catalogue licence fields are null. Portal terms link the document headed
   **ENA Shared Data Licence**. Clauses 1.2/2.1 require Licence Details/Permitted
   Uses; clauses 3.1.4 and 3.1.6–3.1.8 restrict derivation, combination,
   redistribution and commercial use unless expressly permitted. Corrected the
   blanket CC BY/free-registration treatment; linked exact base terms and tagged
   both CIM datasets **research-only / use on hold**. Actual Licence Details are
   **UNVERIFIED**. Tabular SPEN sources remain CC BY 4.0.

4. **High — Seven original circuit bundles cannot be confirmed anonymously.**
   UKPN (three areas), SPEN (two) and Northern Powergrid (two) returned structured
   `403 ForbiddenAccess` for circuit records. Schemas are public; voltage/area
   content was not inspected. Marked **UNVERIFIED**, not independently verified.
   No login was attempted, and no restriction was bypassed.

5. **Medium — ENWL payload formats unresolved: one original format bundle.**
   Public pages confirm secure access, November 2025 revision, 132 kV-to-primary-
   busbar scope, circuits/transformers/diagrams and steady-state CIM models, but not
   the asserted XLSX/CSV/XML/PDF payload list. Actual formats/units are
   **UNVERIFIED**. Its introduction links the licence, legally headed
   **ELECTRICITY NORTH WEST SHARED DATA LICENCE**, hosted on **SP ENW Shared Licence**.
   The existing page-title alias/restricted status was valid, not counted as an error.

6. **High — 28 transformer/substation checks added, outside the error denominator.**
   Added UKPN Tables 2a/2b and Grid/Primary Sites; NGED Tables 2a/2b and Attachment 8;
   SSEN workbook evidence; SPEN transformer/abbreviation tables; Northern Powergrid
   Appendix 4/EHV Sites; and ENWL advertised scope. Gated content stays
   **UNVERIFIED**, including Scottish interface transformers. GSP names and node/
   abbreviation tables are not complete geospatial or ownership-verified registers.

7. **Medium — Filter/schema fixes, supplementary.** UKPN uses `licencearea`, not
   `dno`; Northern Powergrid circuits/transformers use `license`, CIM uses
   `licence_area`. NGED two-winding Table 2a uses **`WMID`**, versus `WMIDS` in
   circuit/three-winding files. Correct resource-specific filtering avoids losing
   West Midlands assets. Gated area-value lists remain **UNVERIFIED**.

8. **Medium — Dates/units narrowed to their evidence, supplementary.** NGED N1
   package modification is July 3, 2026, not July 28; inspected electrical resources
   are dated December 3, 2025. N2 metadata is May 29, 2026. Current UKPN CIM metadata
   is October 19, 2026, not the tabular June 29 timestamp. None is automatically a
   file edition date. NGED CSV headers do not establish ampere ratings/impedance
   base; inspected UKPN circuit metadata does not establish its 100 MVA base.
   Marked **UNVERIFIED**. SPEN schemas distinguish SPM percentages from SPD per-unit;
   SEPD headers explicitly establish percentage/100 MVA impedances and MVA ratings.

9. **High — Completeness assertion withdrawn, supplementary.** Of 4,183 NGED
   Table 1 rows operating at 132 kV, 3,045 have a blank/`-` in at least one of
   R1/X1/B1/Summer/Winter. These include switching equipment, not necessarily bad
   physical-line parameters, but neither schema nor licence proves a complete
   simulation-ready network. Gated records cannot establish completeness either.

10. **Medium — SHEPD not 33-kV-only; absence test corrected, supplementary.**
    Table 1 contains 2,139 rows at 33 kV and 56 at 11 kV, none at 132 kV. Table 2
    has no 132 kV winding cells. The previous shared-string-only check was
    insufficient: XLSX numeric voltages need not be in `sharedStrings.xml`.
    Replaced that reasoning with cell-level checks; site/ownership inference is separate.
