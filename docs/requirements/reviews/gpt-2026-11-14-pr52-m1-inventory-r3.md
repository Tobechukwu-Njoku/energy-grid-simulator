## Demand identity check

The [NESO historic-demand package](https://api.neso.energy/api/3/action/package_show?id=historic-demand-data), resource `f6d02c0f-957b-48cb-82ee-09003f2ba759` for 2024, describes ND as metered generation excluding station load, hydro-storage pumping and interconnector exports. Its TSD definition restores all three categories. Thus the corrected working identity is:

`Gross Underlying Demand (declared starting boundary) = ND + embedded solar + embedded wind`

There is no source basis for deducting pumping again. In the first row of the [committed sample](../../../spikes/data/samples/demand-2024.csv), this gives `21,783 + 0 + 2,804 = 24,587 MW`, or `12,293.5 MWh` over that half-hour. The former subtraction would remove an extra `808 MW` / `404 MWh`. This arithmetic checks the specific correction; it does not demonstrate a validated full-year end-use reconstruction. The inventory correctly leaves station-load treatment, transmission losses, import/export boundaries, battery/storage accounting, allocation and energy-integration fixtures unresolved. Do not equate this working boundary with every possible definition of gross consumption.

## Remaining correction and non-blocking wording

1. **[med] R3-01 — R2-05 retains a publisher-count mismatch.** [Inventory](../../research/m1-data-inventory.md), lines 84, 90–123, 928 and 940. A line-based recount gives 54 unique `SRC-*` rows, exactly 29 open-commercial and 25 research-only (`29/54 = 53.7%`, `25/54 = 46.3%`, rounded to one decimal). The publisher mapping assigns every external ID exactly once, but contains **32 numbered buckets**, including the DNO collective at item 32, not 31. Moreover, buckets such as SEMO, SEMO & SEMOpx and collective interconnector/DNO publishers are not a deduplicated count of distinct legal entities. **Suggested correction:** call these **32 publisher/resource groups**, explicitly a grouping convention rather than a legal-entity census, and update the total, heading, explanation and response claims consistently; alternatively supply a genuine entity-level normalisation and recompute it. Keep the correct 54/29/25/two-internal totals. Until corrected, mark R2-05 partial and qualify §6's claim that all partial findings are resolved. This is a bounded inventory-summary correction, not a licence or demand-accounting blocker; no acquisition is needed.

2. **[low; non-blocking] R3-N01 — abbreviated TSD wording.** [Inventory](../../research/m1-data-inventory.md), line 534. This sentence names only station load and pumping when describing what TSD adds; the executive backlog and R2-02 response correctly include exports. **Suggested change:** add interconnector exports here too, matching the publisher definition and the other occurrences. The corrected ND-based identity does not depend on this omission and is not invalidated by it.

3. **[low; non-blocking] R3-N02 — response overstates metering completion.** [Inventory](../../research/m1-data-inventory.md), response R2-03 at line 938. It says explicit sending/receiving metering conventions were added, while the actual ledger correctly labels metering unverified and the paragraph leaves it missing. **Suggested change:** say forward/reverse geography was specified and metering-boundary fields were explicitly left unverified. The honest ledger/backlog satisfies R2-03; the response should not imply that the convention itself is sourced.
