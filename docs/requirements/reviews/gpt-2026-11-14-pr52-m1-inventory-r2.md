## Remaining findings and suggested corrections

1. **[high] R2-01 — UK ETS still has an invented clearance label.** Inventory lines 61 and 593 (`SRC-36`/`SRC-36a`); F-01/F-10, FR-19.AC1. The only UKA URL is acknowledged as a 404, yet the entry still assigns OGL v3.0 and `open-commercial`. There is no identified release, original auction-data rights holder or grant supporting that upgrade. Government hosting or an OGL site footer cannot clear third-party auction observations. **Correction:** retain `research-only` with licence unverified and PC-5 until the exact dataset-level grant is established; identify release/owner/units/currency/frequency separately from the licence. The combined register row must reflect the same safe disposition. No acquisition is required to correct this assertion.

2. **[high] R2-02 — the new demand equation double-subtracts pumping.** Inventory lines 492–494; F-03, FR-3.AC3/AC4, NFR-2. The official [NESO historic-demand package](https://api.neso.energy/api/3/action/package_show?id=historic-demand-data), in its 2024 resource description, defines ND as already excluding station load, hydro-storage pumping and interconnector exports; TSD adds these back. Consequently `ND + embedded solar + embedded wind - pumping` is not justified by that source. The [committed demand sample](../../../spikes/data/samples/demand-2024.csv) starts with ND 21,783 MW, embedded wind 2,804 MW, solar 0 and pumping 808 MW: the new formula removes another 808 MW (404 MWh in that half-hour). **Correction:** remove the unverified equation or derive a source-consistent definition with explicit station-load/loss/import/export boundaries. Record pumping/storage withdrawal once, and leave reconciliation/MWh/double-counting fixtures explicitly pending. `ND + embedded estimates` is a starting identity, not proof that all gross-demand/loss accounting is resolved.

3. **[med] R2-03 — loss ledger introduces ten unsupported numeric defaults.** Inventory lines 512–526 and response F-05; F-05, FR-1.AC3. Per-link estimates such as IFA 2.5%, IFA2 2.3% and Greenlink 2.8% are new, uncited values, not sourced coefficients or reproducible engineering approximations. “Estimated” and a MISSING label do not establish a derivation. The ledger supplies neither sending/receiving conventions nor distinct directional validity/units, although the response says it does. **Correction:** replace the percentages with MISSING, or document derivation, scope, effective interval, direction, units and confidence. Mark every unfilled ledger field missing; preserve converter/cable separation and the no-double-counting requirement. If introducing actual modelling assumptions, follow the repository's `ASSUMPTION:`/questionnaire rule rather than silently assigning defaults.

4. **[med] R2-04 — evidence/access claims still exceed their cited artifacts.** Inventory lines 283, 302–310, 424, 436, 709–715 and 756; F-09/F-14. The [Elexon coverage](../../../spikes/data-gathering/elexon/COVERAGE.md) and [manifest](../../../spikes/data-gathering/elexon/manifest.toml) evidence acquired flow series, not a hash-verified `/reference/interconnectors/all` snapshot. [ADR 0006's evidence limits](../../architecture/adr/0006-market-design-plugin-model.md#primary-references-and-evidence-limits) explicitly say no specific BSC clause or complete historical rules archive was verified; [SEMO coverage](../../../spikes/data-gathering/semo-2024/COVERAGE.md) analyses archives/settlement cadence, not the cited Part B text. Yet these are still “Rules documented,” “Analysed,” or “Documented / Analysed in Spikes.” The TEC URL named `tec-register-10-november-2026.csv` now redirects to a signed object named `tec-register-13-november-2026.csv`; its filename/UUID is not an immutable November 10 release lock. Meanwhile the matrix promises row exports after login and CIM receipt through SPEN's still-unresolved request form. **Correction:** link the exact capture/analysis if one exists; otherwise downgrade to source identified/reference-only. Distinguish TEC's verified cached November 10 hash from its mutable live resource. Describe accounts as prerequisites, with permissions, request location, approval and payload availability still unverified. Do not require a credential action to close a documentation overclaim. Retain D-65/NFR-12.AC1/AC2 tests before heavy jobs.

5. **[med] R2-05 — source register totals remain unauditable.** Inventory lines 22–81 and 592–598; F-10/F-18. The introduction says external IDs stop at `SRC-34`, while the table has `SRC-01`–`SRC-47`. Counting those 47 rows as written gives **26 open-only, 19 research-only and two mixed** (`SRC-16`, `SRC-36`), not 25/22. Even counting every mixed row conservatively gives 26/21 before correcting UKA. The table contains 30 distinct publisher-column labels, several combined/multi-owner, with no normalisation rule explaining 22 publishers. `SRC-36a/b` and `SRC-37a/b` exist in detail but not as independently counted register rows; CfD and RO remain combined without an actual Ofgem reference. ETYS Appendix G/DNO Table 7 and the TLM candidate also sit outside the supposed exhaustive register. **Correction:** define family versus resource counting, split mixed grants into separately tagged resources, map combined publishers explicitly, add missing candidate/backlog entries and recompute totals from those rows. Add a real Ofgem source reference or mark that field's location unidentified. Do not repair these numbers with another unexplained aggregate.

6. **[med] R2-06 — Greenlink's unverified causal interpretation survives.** Inventory lines 522 and 752, plus response F-07 at line 784; F-07. The executive summary correctly says 7,102 sparse records do not establish commissioning tests. Nevertheless the loss ledger says “Test operations only in 2024,” the completeness table says “sparse test flows,” and the response repeats “sparse test records.” No dated commissioning evidence is supplied. **Correction:** use “7,102 sparse observations; cause unverified; not a fully commissioned 2024 baseline” consistently. Source any commissioning-date claim independently. Count/coverage evidence alone does not identify operational cause.

7. **[low] R2-07 — scenario-input inventory and closure summary need completion.** Inventory lines 585–589 and §6; F-15/F-18. Calling ADR 0002 conceptual and listing unimplemented schema/locks/fixtures fixes the implementation overclaim, but does not inventory several identity inputs requested in round 1. **Correction:** add a short missing-artifact checklist or an explicit cross-reference to ADR 0002's scenario/input/execution identities, knowledge mode, semantic canonicalisation, effective/publication/acquisition times, calendar/transformation/cost/FX/proxy versions, rights lineage and migration/hash vectors. No code is requested. Change §6 to reflect partial findings until their documentation corrections are actually present; do not equate “backlog acknowledged” with a verified source, parameter or implementation.
